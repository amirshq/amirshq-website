About me

From research to production AI

I'm a Senior AI/ML Engineer and Data Scientist based in Montreal, with a PhD in Engineering from ÉTS. Most of my work is applied AI that goes into production: RAG systems, LLM orchestration, agentic AI, and classical ML for prediction. I've worked across the full ML lifecycle, from research and data exploration through deployment, monitoring, and MLOps/LLMOps. I work in English and French.

Background

How I got here

I started in research. As an ML researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Sweden, I worked on machine learning for district heating and urban energy systems, and I've published in the energy and ML fields. That's where I learned to work carefully with messy real-world data and to check that a model actually holds up.

From there I moved into industry. As a Data Scientist at TELUS Digital AI Data Solutions I worked on production ML and data work. After that I was AI Engineer & Data Scientist at NAVADA, and I'm now an AI Engineer at SBI Group, delivering AI projects for clients in mining and natural resources.

Key projects

Work that shipped

SBI · Dhilmar

Agentic RAG assistant for a mining client

I designed, built and deployed a client-facing agentic RAG system. I worked with an ML engineer, a business analyst, a project manager, and data and solution architects to deliver it.

Used by 700 engineers, each of whom used to spend one to two hours a day searching documents by hand.

SBI

Process-prediction ML for a gold mine grinding circuit

Part of a soft-sensor proof of concept to predict P80 (particle size) in Snowflake. I built the XGBoost model, with LightGBM as a baseline, worked with metallurgists on data quality issues, and helped define the target ML architecture for production.

Medallion layers, champion/challenger models, and separate DEV and PROD environments.

NAVADA

Technical document assistant

An on-premises agentic RAG assistant for HVAC energy engineers and estimators. It answers questions with source references across large sets of PDFs, Word files and scanned documents. It also includes a tool that categorizes 50,000 to 80,000 items per project and fills in missing categories by looking up part numbers in the supplier database.

To fix hallucinations, I worked through the pipeline one layer at a time: embeddings, retrieval tuning, a cross-encoder reranker with a relevance threshold, and LLM comparison. I validated it against a golden dataset that the company's engineers checked.

Before it existed, 10 to 15 engineers spent two to three weeks per project on this work.

Client work

Demand forecasting

LightGBM and XGBoost forecasting models for retail clients, including promotion detection and correcting sales when items were out of stock.

Side projects

Cortex and Pulsar

Cortex is a RAG assistant with memory that I built twice, once in plain Python and once in LangGraph, to compare the two approaches (see the comparison). Pulsar is a Telegram bot that sends AI news and runs on a VPS that I deployed and maintain.
